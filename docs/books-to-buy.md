# Old dictionaries to buy (French, German, Italian)

English needs no books: the free texts are already in the pipeline.

**The date that works:** every author, editor and reviser named on the title
page died before 1 January 1956 (UK, EU, Switzerland; the year moves on each
January), and for US sales the book was printed in 1930 or earlier.

Before paying: ask for photos of the title page and its back (year, any
"revised by" name), and a shipping quote to Switzerland (these books are heavy).

| Priority | Book | Edition to look for | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Eberhard, *Synonymisches Handwörterbuch der deutschen Sprache* | 17th ed., 1910, edited by Otto Lyon (English/French/Italian equivalents in every group) | print-on-demand reprints with an ISBN; early-1800s editions without translations |
| 2 | *Petit Larousse illustré* / *Nouveau Petit Larousse illustré* | any printing 1906–1930 | undated copies, 1931 and later |
| 3 | Zingarelli, *Vocabolario della lingua italiana* | Bietti (& Reggiani), 1922, 1925, 1926 or 1928 | 1935 and later, Zanichelli, "Edizione minore" |
| later | Lafaye, *Dictionnaire des synonymes de la langue française* | printed 1923 or earlier | René Bailly's dictionary (still in copyright) |
| later | Schlessing, *Deutscher Wortschatz* | 1881 or 1903 (Schlessing alone) | anything with Wehrle or Eggers |
| later | Tommaseo, *Nuovo dizionario dei sinonimi* | 19th-century printing | |
| later | Rigutini–Fanfani, *Vocabolario italiano della lingua parlata* | 1875 or later printings to 1930 | |
